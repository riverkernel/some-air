Get it on Android

Not on Google Play yet. You can install the app directly:

Download Some Air 1.1.1 (APK)

  1. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser; allow it once.
  2. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway; the app makes no network requests at all.

Updates don't arrive automatically this way. Check back here for new versions, or email us and we'll let you know.

Support

Something not working, or a pattern you'd like added? Email riverkernel@gmail.com. Include your phone model and iOS version if it's a bug.

What it does

  • Ten breathing patterns from the classic chart: 4‑7‑8, Box, 5‑5, Slow box, 6‑3‑6‑3, 6‑6, 3‑3‑6, 4‑6, 7‑11 and 2‑4.
  • A dot walks the pattern's shape, one side per phase, with a soft tone and a tap at each corner.
  • Streak and weekly minutes, kept on your phone.
  • Dark or light, count up or down, tones and haptics on or off.
