What we collect

Nothing. I Need Some Air does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that phone home.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your settings (theme, count direction, tones, haptics, rounds) and a log of your breathing sessions (when, how long, which pattern) so it can show your streak and weekly minutes. This is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works entirely offline.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It keeps the screen awake during a session and uses the phone's vibration for haptic cues; neither requires a permission prompt.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
